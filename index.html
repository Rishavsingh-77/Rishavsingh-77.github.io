Project

CampusResell: demand test for a student resale marketplace

Individual business project · pilot survey, 20 responses

Problem: students on a large residential campus buy and discard the same items (books, cycles, appliances, electronics) every semester, with no trusted way to trade them.

What I did: I designed a 10-question anonymous survey for LPU students and analysed the 20 responses in Python (pandas, matplotlib).

Findings: 65% would use a student-only resale platform and another 25% said maybe. Today most trading runs through WhatsApp groups (65%) and friends (30%). Campus pickup is the top trust factor (65%). However, 19 of 20 would pay nothing per sale, so a commission model is not supported and revenue would need to come from elsewhere, such as featured listings or local business partnerships.

Limits: small convenience sample collected quickly, so this is a pilot rather than proof of market size. One question was set up wrongly and is excluded.
